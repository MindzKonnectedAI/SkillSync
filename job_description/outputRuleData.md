# Job Posting: Sr Java Developer II (2799)

**Location:** Taylor, TX
**Duration:** Contract

## Job Description

The Applications Developer will collaborate with ERCOT business partners to develop and support complex business applications and tools for the Settlements and Credit Application Services team. We are looking for a talented Java Developer to join our team, focusing on both front-end and back-end components of web applications.

## Job Summary

- **Design and Development:**
- Design, code, test, document, and maintain applications to meet ERCOT business needs based on the defined Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).
- Collaborate with customers, business area subject matter experts, team members, and other internal technical teams to develop and maintain complex ERCOT applications.

- **Technical Compliance:**
- Utilize toolsets that adhere to the frameworks of the technical stack used across the Application Services division.
- Ensure that products and solutions comply with the organization’s architectural standards.

- **Problem Solving:**
- Address moderately complex issues requiring in-depth evaluation of variable factors.
- Resolve issues creatively and effectively.

- **Product Development Life Cycle:**
- Conduct all phases of the product development life cycle, including analysis, design, testing, and integration.
- Participate in component and data architecture design, performance monitoring, product evaluation, and buy vs. build recommendations.

- **Documentation and Metrics:**
- Provide input for the development of product user manuals.
- Supply code statistics and development metrics (e.g., development cycle time).
- Assist in implementing and maintaining a standardized development methodology.

- **Research and Innovation:**
- Research, evaluate, and incorporate new technologies and tools to enhance the product development process.
- Develop and revise product user manuals.

## Preferred Functional/Technical Skills

- **Java Proficiency:**
- Strong proficiency in Java/J2EE, with a preference for Java 17 or greater.
