### Job Title
- **Position:** Solution Architect

### Location
- **City:** Gurugram, India
- **Employment Type:** Full Time

### Key Responsibilities
- **Design and Architecture:**
  - Define, document, and promote high-level design and software architecture.
  - Align architecture with Wink’s business objectives and customer needs.
  
- **Guidance and Review:**
  - Provide architectural guidance to the technology team.
  - Review deliverables for design compliance.
  
- **Technology Evaluation:**
  - Evaluate technology alternatives.
  - Recommend technology components, frameworks, and open-source libraries.
  
- **Non-Functional Requirements:**
  - Define non-functional requirements for the solution.
  - Review final implementation for compliance.

- **Operational Models:**
  - Help define operational and customer onboarding models.
  
- **Collaboration:**
  - Work with Wink’s product teams to create solutions for client needs.
  - Address feasibility questions: “Can this be done? How hard is it?”

- **Sales Support:**
  - Act as a client-facing Wink Platform Expert.
  - Advise on integration with Wink platform and address security/scalability questions.

- **IP Development:**
  - Support IP development and patent filing documentation.

### What You Need
- **Technical Expertise:**
  - Mastery of .NET Core (must have experience as developer, designer, and architect).
  
- **Cloud and Microservices:**
  - Significant expertise in Cloud Native, Microservices, and API-based web applications.
  
- **Cloud Provider Experience:**
  - Experience with at least one major cloud computing provider (preferably Azure).
  
- **Azure Skills:**
  - Strong skills in Azure communication protocols and scalability.

- **Product Development:**
  - Experience in building and launching new products/platforms (must, not just updates).

- **Security and Performance:**
  - Familiarity with security principles, high availability designs, and performance optimization.
