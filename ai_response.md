**Job Title: Sr. Java Developer II**

**Overview:**
We are seeking a highly skilled and motivated Sr. Java Developer II to join our dynamic team. This role is critical in developing robust Java applications that meet our business needs while ensuring compliance with architectural standards. The ideal candidate will possess a strong technical background, excellent problem-solving abilities, and a passion for innovation.

---

**Role Summary:**
As a Sr. Java Developer II, you will be responsible for designing, coding, testing, documenting, and maintaining applications throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC). You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality software solutions that enhance our product offerings.

---

**Responsibilities:**
- Design, code, test, document, and maintain applications to meet business needs based on the defined SDLC.
- Ensure that products and solutions comply with the organization’s architectural standards.
- Address moderately complex issues where analysis requires an in-depth evaluation of variable factors and resolve issues creatively and effectively.
- Conduct all phases of the product development lifecycle, including analysis, design, testing, and integration of products.
- Provide input and insights for the development of product user manuals and supply code statistics and development metrics (e.g., development cycle time).
- Research, evaluate, and incorporate new technologies and tools to enhance the product development process.

---

**Required Qualifications:**
- Proficiency in Java/J2EE, preferably Java 17 or greater.
- Hands-on experience in developing Java applications.
- Strong understanding of Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) principles and implementing solutions using RDBMS.
- Proficiency in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and jQuery.
- Proficiency with JSP / Spring or Struts2.
- Proficiency with ORM / Hibernate and RDBMS / SQL.
- Experience with Oracle PL/SQL.
- Ability to determine the best performance optimization for a given requirement.
- Self-starter and motivated individual who enjoys working outside their comfort zone.
- Team player with the ability to work collaboratively with other team members.
- Strong problem-solving and communication skills.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
- Ability to identify patterns from problem definitions.
- Ability to work in an environment that requires quick turnaround when troubleshooting production issues.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, or an equivalent combination of education and experience.
- Minimum of 5 years of related work experience in excess of degree requirements.

---

**Preferred Qualifications:**
- Experience with Linux operating systems.
- Familiarity with version control systems, particularly Git.
- Knowledge of build tools such as Maven.
- Experience with Continuous Integration/Deployment tools like Jenkins.
- Familiarity with reporting tools such as Jasper Reports.
- Experience with testing frameworks like JUnit.
- Knowledge of grid management tools such as ParamQuery Grid (PQGrid).
- Experience with web servers like Tomcat / JBoss Web Server.
- Familiarity with containerization technologies, particularly Docker Containers.
