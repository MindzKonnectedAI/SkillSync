# Job Title: Microsoft Dynamics Developer

**Location:** Gurgaon, NCR, India
**Employment Type:** Full-Time

## Position Summary
The Microsoft Dynamics CRM Developer will be responsible for participating at an intermediate level in all phases of the development and maintenance life cycle for Microsoft Dynamics CRM at ALLDATA. The ideal candidate will have hands-on experience with Dynamics CRM customization, C# programming, configuration (including UI/form development for web and unified client interface), Microsoft Flows, custom development (including Unified Service Desk), web resources, data integrations, and CRM solution file management. The candidate must have the ability to manage and prioritize multiple demands from a large user base across various departments. A deep knowledge of Dynamics 365 CRM in all functional and technical areas is essential.

## Key Responsibilities
- Participate in design sessions with key business users to gather and document business processes and requirements for the system.
- Architect the CRM system, related customizations, and reports to model the business organizational process.
- Design and develop D365 CDS PowerApps model, automating business processes with Microsoft Flow.
- Design and develop Azure Data Factory pipelines and flows.
- Build and configure CRM forms, views, dashboards, and workflows.
- Build plugins, manage solution deployments, and perform debugging.
- Develop reports using FetchXML.
- Balance business users' requests and requirements with technical constraints.
- Collaborate with internal support members to resolve technical issues with servers and other related technologies in the internal CRM system.
- Maintain code repository and possess working knowledge of GitHub for source control.
- Effectively utilize SDK for administration of the CRM system.
- Provide support for the application, including fixing application issues.

## Required Skills and Qualifications
- Hands-on experience with Microsoft Dynamics CRM customization and development.
- Proficiency in C# programming.
- Experience with Dynamics 365 CRM, including functional and technical aspects.
- Familiarity with Microsoft Flows and PowerApps.
- Knowledge of Azure Data Factory.
- Experience in building and configuring CRM forms, views, dashboards, and workflows.
- Ability to manage multiple demands and prioritize effectively.
- Strong problem-solving skills and ability to work collaboratively with teams.
