# Job Posting: Senior Software Engineer at SRS Acquiom

## About SRS Acquiom
SRS Acquiom focuses on simplifying complex transactions in M&A and Loan Agency markets. Since 2007, we have been innovating to address challenges for sophisticated buyers, sellers, borrowers, lenders, and counsels. Our team has supported over 7,000 deals with an aggregate value of $815 billion, including notable transactions such as:
- Coca Cola Corporation’s acquisition of Body Armor
- Pfizer’s acquisition of ReViral Limited
- Intel’s acquisition of Granulate Cloud Solutions

We are seeking extraordinary individuals to contribute to our ongoing success. If you are interested in joining a growing, entrepreneurial environment within an established company, we want to hear from you!

## Position Summary
SRS Acquiom is looking for a contract-to-hire Senior Software Engineer and technology leader to work with a dedicated, cross-functional agile product team in a diverse, fast-paced environment. This position is remote, with the option to work from our office in Denver, CO.

### Team Dash
We are responsible for the SRS public-facing Deal Dashboard web application, where deal participants can view all aspects of a merger or acquisition. Our work is critical to our customers, requiring accuracy and timeliness. We maintain high standards and produce high-quality results.

### Technologies We Use

We organize our work using Jira, build and deploy with Bitbucket and Jenkins, and have automated test coverage, allowing us to deploy multiple times per day.
